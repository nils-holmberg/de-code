PRDs, MVP specifications, acceptance criteria, and chat prompts are becoming executable interfaces. Two ways into the paradigm

From written intent to working systems.

Both courses treat specifications as active working material: documents that guide AI-assisted building, make assumptions visible, and give teams something concrete to review and test.

For academics

Specification-driven research tools

Turn research questions and methodological requirements into clear specifications for analyses, reproducible workflows, teaching tools, and small research applications.

  • Move from research question to PRD, workflow, and acceptance criteria.
  • Use prompts and AI agents to build and revise an MVP.
  • Test outputs for validity, traceability, and reproducibility.

For public organizations

From service need to governed prototype

Translate policy, service, and operational needs into specifications that multidisciplinary teams can inspect before AI-assisted tools are built or procured.

  • Frame needs as user stories, constraints, and measurable outcomes.
  • Prototype internal tools through structured prompts and MVP specs.
  • Document responsibility, data boundaries, and quality checks.
